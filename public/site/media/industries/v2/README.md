## Gemeinsamer Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.

Asset type: full-bleed website industry-card key visual, landscape 16:10.

Create premium photorealistic cinematic Swiss spatial-media imagery with
high-end architectural lifestyle photography, editorial realism, authentic
people and materials, understated luxury, a 35mm eye-level camera and subtle
film grain. Integrate digital displays naturally into the architecture. Keep
the lower third quiet and dark enough for large white website typography.
Use charcoal, natural stone, warm timber and restrained Swiss red accents.
Create one coherent scene only: no collage and no split screen. Display
content must remain abstract and contain no readable words, numbers, logos,
trademarks or interface text. No watermark, caption or border. Avoid staged
stock photography, excessive neon, oversaturation, duplicated people,
distorted anatomy and fake AI typography.

## Motivspezifische Prompts

### Retail

A sophisticated contemporary fashion store where two adult customers
naturally discover products while a tall integrated digital display presents
elegant abstract product imagery. Refined dark timber, stone, brushed metal,
real clothing and footwear displays, warm practical lights and controlled
cinematic contrast.

### Gastronomie

A high-end modern European restaurant where guests naturally interact while
an architecturally integrated digital menu display presents elegant abstract
food imagery. Dark timber, natural stone, brushed metal, an open kitchen and
warm evening practical lights.

### Hospitality

A refined contemporary alpine city-hotel lobby where adult travellers are
naturally welcomed at reception while a large integrated media wall displays
calm abstract alpine destination imagery. Warm stone, dark oak, brushed brass
and soft evening light.

### Health

A calm contemporary Swiss clinic or health-centre reception where an adult
visitor is naturally greeted by a professional while an integrated digital
information wall provides orientation through abstract colour fields and a
map-like graphic. Warm timber, light mineral surfaces and soft daylight.

### Corporate

A contemporary executive collaboration room where a diverse small team
discusses a project while a large integrated wall display shows elegant
abstract data visualisation. Glass, dark timber, acoustic surfaces and a stone
table with focused, controlled light.

### Public

A contemporary Swiss public mobility or civic hall where adults naturally
navigate the space while an integrated digital wayfinding wall shows abstract
map lines and colour-coded routes. Stone, timber, glass and restrained
daylight.

### Events

A sophisticated product-launch or cultural-event venue where a restrained
audience experiences a large architectural LED stage. Abstract white and
Swiss-red light fields extend into the room with controlled cinematic
volumetric light and deep blacks.

### Experience

An immersive museum or brand-experience room where a few visitors explore
panoramic projection and LED surfaces. The architecture becomes an abstract
alpine light landscape made from particles, contour lines and a restrained
Swiss-red path. Atmospheric, contemplative and free of literal attractions.
